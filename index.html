Learn the Japanese Te-form for Travel

20 minutes + practice · English-speaking beginners

Beginners freeze when they need to ask for something in Japanese. This web lesson, built with an AI coding assistant, teaches the te-form (a key step in Japanese grammar) through real travel requests (〜てください). Any instructor can teach it the same way on one shared screen.

  • Aims for about 70% learner speaking time.
  • Japanese checked for naturalness, beginner level and on-screen readability.

How Much Salt Is in My Favorite Food?

Healthy eating for kids · 30-minute online lesson · elementary & junior high

Kids love fast food, but “don’t eat that” never works. Kids add up the salt in their favorite meal, compare it with the daily limit for their age, and learn habits that help prevent high blood pressure. It starts from a question kids really ask: “Why won’t adults let me eat fast food three times a day?”

  • A 30-second animation shows how extra salt affects blood vessels.
  • The lesson ends with “helper foods” to add, not foods to give up.

A Life That Honors Your Inner Self

Workshop for adults · self-care + AI literacy

Many adults never learned to take care of themselves. Learners notice what makes them feel happy all day, turn it into clear conditions, and use AI to explore a life, and a town, built around it. First tried in a mixed-grade online class for students.

  • AI suggests three towns in Japan and three abroad; the group checks them together.
  • No personal info, every answer optional, and AI ideas treated as “candidates, not answers.”
