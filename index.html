Salt & Health Lesson for Kids

30-minute online lesson · mixed grades (elementary & junior high)

Built around a question kids really ask: “Why won’t adults let me eat fast food three times a day?”

  • Students pick their dream fast-food dinner and add up the salt in a web tool.
  • They compare it with the daily target for their own age group.
  • A 30-second animation shows how extra salt affects blood vessels.
  • The lesson ends with “helper foods” to add, not foods to give up.

Find a Town That Fits What You Love

Online lesson · mixed grades · AI literacy

Students turn vague likes into clear conditions, then use AI to explore towns that fit them.

  • Starts with one adult’s real example: likes and dislikes, a talk with AI, and real town suggestions.
  • Students collect their own likes at their own pace in a web tool.
  • AI suggests three towns in Japan and three abroad; the class checks them together.
  • No personal info, every answer optional, and AI ideas treated as “candidates, not answers.”
